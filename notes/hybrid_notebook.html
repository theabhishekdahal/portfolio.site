Question 1 · OCD — Basic Calculations
The data given below relates to a convertible bond:
Face valueRs. 250
Coupon rate12%
No. of shares per bond20
Market price of shareRs. 12
Straight value of bondRs. 235
Market price of convertible bondRs. 265
Calculate: (i) Stock value of bond. (ii) % of downside risk. (iii) Conversion premium. (iv) Conversion parity price of stock.
Ans: (i) Rs. 240  (ii) 11.32%  (iii) 10.42%  (iv) Rs. 13.25
(i) Stock value of bond
= MP of share × Conversion ratio = 12 × 20 = Rs. 240
(ii) Percentage of downside risk
= MP of OCD − Straight valueMP of OCD × 100 = 265 − 235265 × 100 = 11.32%
(iii) Conversion premium
= CPP − MPMP × 100 = (265/20) − 1212 × 100 = 13.25 − 1212 × 100 = 10.42%
(iv) Conversion parity price of stock
= MP of OCDMP of shares × Conversion ratio = 26520 = Rs. 13.25
✦ What makes this question special

Foundational OCD question. Four key ratios: Stock Value = MPS × CR. Downside risk = how far MP can fall before hitting straight value. CPP = MP of OCD ÷ CR. Conversion premium = (CPP − MPS)/MPS × 100.

Question 6 · OCD Valuation — Type 2 (Delta Corporation)
Delta Corporation OCD details:
Face valueRs. 1000
Life8 years
Coupon rate10%
YTM on similar NCD15%
Conversion Ratio5
Market Price of ShareRs. 150
(i) Expected market value if option premium = Rs. 30. (ii) Expected market value if MPS expected to reach Rs. 250 in 8 years.
Ans: (i) Rs. 805.63  (ii) Rs. 857.36
Part (i)
Step 1: Calc of straight / investment value
= PV of all future cash flows associated with bond
= (1000×10%) × PVIFA(15%, 8yr) + 1000 × PVIF(15%, 8yr)
= 100 × 4.48732 + 1000 × 0.32690
= Rs. 775.632
Step 2: Calc of conversion value
= Conversion ratio × MP per share = 5 × 150 = Rs. 750
Step 3: Floor value = Higher of (Step 1, Step 2) = Higher of (775.632, 750) = 775.632
Step 4: Expected MV = Floor value + Option premium = 775.632 + 30 = Rs. 805.632
Part (ii)
T. Note: In this part, value of option premium is not given. But expected MP of Delta after 8 years is given, which is a base for calculating value of option premium.
Value of option is the PV of expected benefit from the convertible bond. Convertible option provides an investor a right to choose the following two cashflows after 8 years:
(i) Redemption value of Rs. 1000
(ii) 5 shares of Delta [expected value at t8] = 250 × 5 = 1250
Extra benefit on choosing conversion = 1250 − 1000 = 250
PV of benefit = 250 × PVIF(15%, 8yr) = 250 × 0.32690 = Rs. 81.73
So, value of option premium = Rs. 81.73
Calc of expected value of OCD = Floor value + Option premium = 775.632 + 81.73 = Rs. 857.36
✦ What makes this question special

OCD Valuation: always 4 steps. Floor value = higher of (straight value, conversion value). Option premium in part (ii) must be derived as PV of extra benefit from choosing conversion over redemption. Always use current MPS for conversion value in step 2.

Question 7 · OCD Valuation — Phototech plc (Floor, MV, Premium)
Phototech plc has 9% bonds redeemable at par £100 in 5 years. Each bond may be converted on that date into 20 ordinary shares. Current share price = £4.45, expected to grow at 6.5% p.a. Cost of debt = 7% p.a.
(i) Floor value. (ii) Market value. (iii) Conversion premium.
Ans: (i) £108.20  (ii) £123.84  (iii) 39.15%
Part (i): Floor value
Conversion ratio = 20. MP of share = £4.45
(A) Straight value
= (100×9%) × PVIFA(7%, 5yr) + 100 × PVIF(7%, 5yr)
= 9 × 4.100197 + 100 × 0.71298 = 36.90 + 71.30 = Rs. 108.1997
(B) Conversion value (always use current MPS)
= Conversion ratio × MP of share = 20 × 4.45 = £ 89
Floor value = Higher of (A, B) = Higher of (108.20, 89) = £ 108.20
Part (ii): Market value of OCD
MP of share at end of 5 years = 4.45 × (1+6.5%)⁵ = 4.45 × 1.37009 = 6.09688
Redemption value = £ 100; Expected value from 20 shares (t5) = 6.09688 × 20 = £ 121.9377
Extra benefit on choosing conversion = 121.9377 − 100 = 21.9377
PV of benefit = 21.9377 × PVIF(7%, 5yr) = 21.9377 × 0.71298 = £ 15.641
MV of OCD = Floor value + Option premium = 108.20 + 15.641 = £ 123.84
Part (iii): Conversion premium
= CPP − MPMP × 100 = 123.84/20 − 4.454.45 × 100 = 6.192 − 4.454.45 × 100 = 39.15%
✦ What makes this question special

Share price grows at 6.5% p.a. — must project MPS to t5 to compute option premium. Conversion value for floor (step 2) uses today's MPS. Option premium uses future MPS. This distinction is critical.

Question 8 · OCD Valuation — Reverse Calculation (XYZ Corporation)
Data given for XYZ Corporation convertible bond:
Face valueRs. 1000
Conversion Ratio4
Conversion Parity PriceRs. 300
Current MP of similar NCDRs. 1100
Expected Dividend per shareRs. 10
Option PremiumRs. 50
Premium Payback Period3 years
Calculate: (i) Market Price of OCD  (ii) Market Price of Stock  (iii) Coupon rate of OCD.
Ans: (i) Rs. 1200  (ii) Rs. 287.5  (iii) 5.67%
Part (i): Market Price of OCD
Here, Conversion parity price = 300.   MP of OCD/Conversion ratio = 300   ∴ MP of OCD = 300 × 4 = Rs. 1200
Part (ii): Market Price of OCD = Floor price + Option premium
∴ Floor price = 1200 − 50 = Rs. 1150
As floor price = higher of (a) straight value and (b) conversion value. Straight value = 1100 (given as similar NCD).
So, conversion value = 1150
Conversion ratio × MP per share = 1150   Or, 4 × MP per share = 1150
∴ MP per share = Rs. 287.5
Part (iii): Coupon rate of OCD
Conversion premium price = CPP − MP = 300 − n (where n is coupon rate × 1000/4)
Favourable diff. income = (1000×n%) / 4 − 10 = 2.5n − 10
Premium payback period = 3;   Or, 12.5/(2.5n−10) = 3   [Premium = CPP − MPS = 300 − 287.5 = 12.5]
Or, 12.5 = 3 × (2.5n − 10)   Or, 12.5 = 7.5n − 30   Or, 42.5 = 7.5n
∴ n = 5.67%
✦ What makes this question special

Reverse OCD: work backwards from given CPP and payback period to find MPS and coupon rate. Floor price = MP − option premium. Set up the payback equation: premium per share ÷ (coupon/CR − dividend) = payback period, then solve for n.

CaseMPS todayTotal value from equity CF

Question 11 · CCD — Compulsory Convertible Debenture Valuation
ABC Ltd. issued 5-year 12% coupon paying bond with face value of Rs. 1000. Each bond compulsorily gets converted into 5 equity shares at the end of life of the bond. At that time, each equity share is expected to command a market value of Rs. 150 per share. Compute value of the bond today if investor's required rate is 15% p.a.
Ans: Rs. 775.13
Sol: [Part B — CCD]
Mathematically, value of CCD is computed as:
IV of CCD = PV of all coupon payments till date of conversion + PV of (conversion value expected on the date of conversion)
* Conversion value = Conversion ratio × MPS
Note: Discount rate to be used for computing PV should be reqd rate on similar risk class instrument i.e. similar CCD or equity instrument.
Diagram:
t1 to t5: coupons of Rs. 120 each year
t5: Conversion value = 5 × 150 = Rs. 750 (instead of Rs. 1000 redemption)
Intrinsic value of CCD
= 120 × PVIFA(15%, 5yr) + 750 × PVIF(15%, 5yr)
= 120 × 3.35215 + 750 × 0.49717
= Rs. 775.13
✦ What makes this question special

CCD differs from OCD: conversion is compulsory (no option). The terminal cashflow is the conversion value (CR × expected MPS), NOT the face value Rs. 1000. Discount rate = required rate on similar equity-type instrument (15% here).

Question 12 · NCD with Warrants — Infinity Limited
Infinity Limited issued 6-year, 10%, Rs. 1000 FV bond with a warrant attached. The warrant provides right to buy 5 equity shares of Infinity at an exercise price of Rs. 300 per share in 3 years from now. Expected market price of each equity share at that time is Rs. 400. Find Value of the bond if NCD without warrants are yielding 12%.
Ans: Rs. 1,274
Sol: [Part C — NCD with Warrants]
Warrant = right to buy shares at pre-determined exercise price. If MP of share > exercise price → bondholder will exercise warrant. If MP of share < exercise price → bondholder won't exercise warrant.
Valuation of NCD with warrant: IV of NCD = Step 1 + Step 2
Step 2: Expected MP = Straight value + Option premium
Step 1: Calculation of straight value
= 100 × PVIFA(12%, 6yr) + 1000 × PVIF(12%, 6yr)
= 100 × 4.111407 + 1000 × 0.50663
= Rs. 917.77
Step 2: Calculation of option premium
Benefit due to option to buy equity share = 5 × (400 − 300) = 5 × 100 = Rs. 500
PV of benefit = 500 × PVIF(12%, 3yr) = 500 × 0.71178 = Rs. 355.89
∴ Value of option premium = Rs. 355.89
Hence, IV of NCD with warrant = Step 1 + Step 2
= 917.77 + 355.89 = Rs. 1,273.66 ≈ Rs. 1,274
✦ What makes this question special

NCD with warrant: non-convertible but has an attached call option on equity shares. Option premium = PV of benefit from exercising warrant = PV of (expected MPS − exercise price) × no. of shares. Discounted at NCD yield rate back to t0.
