Question 1 · Type 1 – No Growth / Constant Growth

ABC Ltd. has been maintaining a growth rate of 10 percent in dividends. The company has paid dividend @ Rs. 3 per share. The rate of return on market portfolio is 12 percent and the risk-free rate of return in the market has been observed as 8 percent. The Beta co-efficient of company's share is 1.5.

You are required to calculate the required rate of return on company's shares as per CAPM model and price per share by dividend growth model.

Ans: Rs. 82.50
g = 10%,  D0 = 3,  Rm = 12%,  Rf = 8%,  β = 1.5
Using CAPM calculation of Re:
Re = Rf + β(Rm − Rf)
= 8% + 1.5(12% − 8%)
= 14%
Calculation of intrinsic value of share:
IV0 = D1Re − g = 3(1 + 10%)14% − 10% = Rs. 82.50
✦ What makes this question special

First CAPM + Gordon model combo. D0 is given — always multiply by (1+g) to get D1 before dividing.
